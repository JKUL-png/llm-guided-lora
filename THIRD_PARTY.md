# Third-party material

License texts are in `LICENSES/`. Same sources as in [decomposing-the-gain](https://github.com/JKUL-png/decomposing-the-gain/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY.md), where each was checked against its primary source.

| Path | Source | License | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| `datos/math500_*` | [HuggingFaceH4/MATH-500](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceH4/MATH-500), a 500-problem subset of MATH from [openai/prm800k](https://github.com/openai/prm800k), based on [hendrycks/math](https://github.com/hendrycks/math) | MIT (both upstream repositories) | `MIT-openai-prm800k.txt`, `MIT-hendrycks-math.txt` |
| `datos/math_train_*`, `datos/math_control20*`, `datos/diag40*` | Problems from the MATH training set, [hendrycks/math](https://github.com/hendrycks/math) | MIT | `MIT-hendrycks-math.txt` |
| `datos/lora_bucles.jsonl` | Training examples: MATH training problems (as above) with text generated by [Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B); see its model card for the model's license | MIT for the problems | `MIT-hendrycks-math.txt` |
| `datos/gsm8k_*` | [openai/gsm8k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openai/gsm8k), from [openai/grade-school-math](https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math) | MIT | `MIT-openai-grade-school-math.txt` |
| `datos/ifeval_*` | [google/IFEval](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/IFEval) | Apache 2.0 | `Apache-2.0.txt` |
| `datos/mit_exp8/edits/p50c/manifest.json` | Manifest of the `p50c` edit from [RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs](https://github.com/RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs). Unchanged. | CC BY 4.0 | `CC-BY-4.0.txt` |
| `docs/vendor/katex/` | [KaTeX](https://katex.org). Unchanged. | MIT | `docs/vendor/katex/LICENSE` |

The fixed subsets in `datos/` keep the original problem text and add our own selection and ids.

The Qwen3.5-0.8B weights and the trained adapters are not included.
